# WiBrawl — neon WB

Two simpler WB directions with neon accents restored.

## WB Shift

Angular shared geometry, clipped corners and a neon lime segment. The condensed lettering draws on the energy of racing, team kit and gaming identities.

## WB Flow

Fuller curves and unequal B bowls, with cyan and pink interruptions. A softer, more expressive lettering direction with an anime and gaming influence.

These references guide the lettering's character. No balls, controllers, vehicles, existing anime characters or official sport emblems appear in the artwork.

Both are custom-filled SVG geometry. Each includes an ink-base accent version, a paper-base accent version for dark backgrounds, and ink/paper monochrome versions. Transparent backgrounds; no font dependencies, gradients, blur filters or embedded raster content.

`neon-wb-comparison.png` compares dark, light and tee applications. `applications.png` includes colour reversals, monochrome and 16–64 px studies.

Run `node design/wibrawl-neon-wb/build.mjs` from the repository root.

These are design proposals, not trademark-cleared marks. They are not installed in the storefront.
