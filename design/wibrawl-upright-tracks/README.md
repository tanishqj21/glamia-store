# WiBrawl — upright W tracks

Two track logos using an upright W:

1. **Upright W + B:** W on the left, normal angular B on the right.
2. **Mirrored B + upright W:** mirrored B on the left, upright W on the right.

The W is defined by two low points and a central high point, with both terminals at the top. It is symmetric about its own vertical axis. Horizontal reflection of the complete route places B on the left and W on the right while retaining W's upright orientation.

The W supplies the chicane section, B supplies the two outer sectors, and a lower return connects them into one closed route. Neither route has a crossing or branch junction.

Each includes ink, paper and red SVG masters, a detailed track SVG and a PNG track preview. `upright-track-comparison.png` compares the logos at the same scale and shows the W in red, B in black and connecting return in grey. `applications.png` shows reversals and 16–64 px renders.

Custom SVG paths with transparent backgrounds. The detailed track treatment adds edging and a start-finish stripe for larger displays.

Run `node design/wibrawl-upright-tracks/build.mjs` from the repository root.

These are design proposals, not trademark-cleared marks. They are not installed in the storefront.
