# Wibrawl — two WB directions

## 01: WB Forward

A forward arrow formed from WB. An angular W supplies the tail, while two counters reveal a B in the pointed head. The intent is movement with the initials embedded in the shape.

## 02: WB Club

A compact WB emblem with rounded contours. Its connected W and B share an upright. The intent is a comfortable clothing mark with a more direct reading of the initials.

Both concepts are drawn as filled SVG paths, without fonts, raster images, masks or background plates. The SVG artwork contains only the initials and their geometry; presentation titles are outside the artwork.

Each concept includes an ink master, a paper variant and an optional rust variant. The PNG presentation boards show chest-print and label studies. `applications.png` shows colour reversals and 16–64 px studies.

Rebuild with `node design/wibrawl-wb-concepts/build.mjs` from the repository root.

These are design proposals, not trademark-cleared marks. Neither has been installed in the storefront.
