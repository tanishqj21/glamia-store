# WiBrawl — Mirror Circuit

A circuit formed by a regular angular B on the right and its exact horizontal mirror on the left. The mirror replaces the previous rotated W. Both halves join at the top and bottom to form one uninterrupted closed course.

The mirrored geometry is derived mathematically from the right half about x=176. It is traversed in reverse to produce one continuous SVG path.

Two B shapes are supplied for comparison:

- **Cutback:** clipped upper sector and longer lower return, with eased corners.
- **Offset:** staggered angular straights and sharper corners.

Each has ink, paper and red SVG masters. `mirror-comparison.png` shows them at the same scale, with two-colour construction diagrams and tee studies. `applications.png` shows reversals and 16–64 px renders.

`wibrawl-mirror-track.svg` and `track-preview.png` show the Cutback course as a larger illustration with red-and-white edging and a start-finish stripe.

Run `node design/wibrawl-mirror-circuit/build.mjs` from the repository root to rebuild.

Custom vector paths; no fonts, embedded raster artwork or background plates. This is a proposed identity, not a trademark-cleared mark. It is not installed in the storefront.
