# Wibrawl — motorsport studies

Two comparable SVG concepts, following the direction “sharp, fast, aggressive.”

1. **Overtake:** an arrow-led silhouette with a double-fold W tail and a pointed, open B-inspired head.
2. **Chicane:** an angular WB emblem with staggered heights, an interlocking diagonal and a long lower return.

Both use custom filled paths. No font dependency, background plate or raster content appears in the masters. Negative spaces are transparent. Ink, paper and red variants share exactly the same geometry.

`comparison.png` presents both at the same scale, including tee and woven-label studies. `applications.png` includes reversals and actual 16–64 px renders.

Rebuild with `node design/wibrawl-motorsport/build.mjs` from the repository root.

These are proposals for comparison, not trademark-cleared logos. They are not installed in the storefront.
