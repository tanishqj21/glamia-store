# Wibrawl — Free Flag

A wordless flag silhouette for the collective idea behind “We Brawl.” The direction connects the energy of a crowd with reaching a checkpoint, expressed as a loose, restless piece of fabric. No monogram, wordmark, mascot or literal equipment appears in the logo.

One filled path, one colour, transparent background. The curves suggest cloth in motion. Two unequal edge cuts and a longer lower corner give the silhouette its particular rhythm.

## Files

- `wibrawl-freeflag.svg`: ink master
- `wibrawl-freeflag-paper.svg`: light version for dark backgrounds
- `wibrawl-freeflag-rust.svg`: optional rust version
- `freeflag-preview.png`: concept presentation with tee and label studies
- `logo-preview.png`: enlarged mark
- `applications.png`: colour variants and 16–64 px scale study
- `build.mjs`: reproduces the SVG masters and previews with Sharp

Run `node design/wibrawl-freeflag/build.mjs` from the repository root.

This is a design proposal. It has not undergone trademark clearance and is not installed in the storefront.
