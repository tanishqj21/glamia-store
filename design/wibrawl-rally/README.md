# Wibrawl — Rally

## The brief behind this mark

**We Brawl** gives Wibrawl its collective energy: a shared love of playing, following sports and finding joy in the culture around them. The logo needs character, without a literal character or a collage of equipment and interests.

The design idea is **still in the game**. A rally in sport and another attempt in a game share the same emotional movement: a setback followed by a return. The mark expresses that movement with two dips and two recoveries. Its final arm reaches higher. Its W connects directly to **We**, giving the mark a link to the name without spelling it out.

The broad, slightly rounded form is designed for casual clothing and streetwear. Unequal arms give it forward movement. It works as a single ink, so its identity comes from the shape. The story is a design rationale; the mark does not literally depict each interest or claim that every viewer will read that meaning without context.

## Files

- `wibrawl-rally.svg`: filled-path master in warm ink `#242422`.
- `wibrawl-rally-chalk.svg`: reversed chalk `#F2EEE5` version.
- `wibrawl-rally-ember.svg`: warm ember `#ED5838` accent version.
- `logo-preview.png`: large preview of the master.
- `rally-preview.png`: concept presentation, tee and label placement studies.
- `applications.png`: colour and 16–64 px rendering checks.
- `build.mjs`: original geometry and reproducible preview builder.

All logo masters are transparent SVGs consisting of one filled path. No fonts, images, masks, filters or external resources are needed. The presentation captions are not part of the logo; the concept label and suggested line do not rename the brand. Clothing illustrations are placement studies, not product photographs.

Maintain the square viewBox and proportions, and leave clear space around the mark. For physical printing, confirm the final ink specification with the printer. Rebuild with `node design/wibrawl-rally/build.mjs` from the repository root using the project's Sharp dependency.

This is a new proposal based on the meaning of We Brawl. Previous proposals remain in their folders, and the storefront has not been changed.
